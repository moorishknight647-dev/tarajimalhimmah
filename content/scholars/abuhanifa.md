# Al-Imam al-A'zam Abu Hanifa

* **Name:** al-Nuʿmān ibn Thābit ibn Zūṭā ibn Marzubān 
* **Kunyah:** Abū Ḥanīfa
* **Moniker:** al-Imām al-Aʿẓam
* **Generation:** Tābiʿīn
* **Nasab:** al-Taymī, al-Kūfī
* **Lifespan:** 80 AH – 150 AH 
* **Region:** al-Kūfah, Iraq (Died in Baghdad)
